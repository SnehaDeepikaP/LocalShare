Metric Definitions & Integrity
  • Completed Exchange: A confirmed exchange where the requester and post owner agreed to transfer a resource, verified upon handover.
  • Closed Listing: A post that was closed without a completed exchange (e.g. author cancelled, expired, or removed). Closed posts do not count as completed exchanges.
  • Privacy Guarantee: Aggregated statistics never disclose private addresses, contact numbers, or personal identifying information.
