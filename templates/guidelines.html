Trust & Safety Policy

LocalShare Community Guidelines

Standards for a safe, respectful, and supportive mutual aid community

Core Principles

LocalShare exists to foster neighbourly solidarity, reduce waste, and provide mutual aid. By participating in LocalShare, every member agrees to the following core tenets:

100% Free Sharing

No money, fees, barter credits, or commercial promotions may be requested. All items and services are shared unconditionally as gifts or mutual help.

Kindness & Respect

Treat every community member with dignity, regardless of background. Harassment, discrimination, and derogatory language will result in permanent suspension.

Privacy & Safety

Never post private home addresses, phone numbers, or financial details in public post descriptions. Use the coordinated private exchange mechanism.

Permitted vs. Prohibited Resources

Permitted Resources
  • Books, textbooks, study guides, and school stationery.
  • Clean, wearable clothing, shoes, and outgrown kids' apparel.
  • Packaged, unexpired pantry goods and fresh home-cooked meals (with clear dietary/allergen disclosure).
  • Small household appliances, kitchenware, furniture, and tools in safe working order.
  • Volunteer assistance: tutoring, tech advice, gardening help, senior assistance.
Strictly Prohibited
  • Prescription medications, controlled substances, and medical equipment.
  • Weapons, explosives, hazardous chemicals, or fireworks.
  • Expired food, recalled products, or broken/unsafe electricals.
  • Commercial advertisements, MLM schemes, sales, or loan offers.
  • Adult products, stolen goods, or counterfeit merchandise.

Safe Coordination Best Practices

  • Public Meeting Spots: For handovers, prefer well-lit, public community locations such as community libraries, local cafes, transit stations, or outside school gates during daytime.
  • Inspect Items: Verify the condition of goods upon collection. Do not accept anything that appears damaged, spoiled, or unsafe.
  • Notify if Plans Change: If you cannot make a scheduled meetup, inform the other person promptly through the platform request note.

Reporting & Moderation

Any user can report suspicious listings or unacceptable behavior by clicking the "Report Post" or "Report User" link. Our moderation team reviews all reports in the administrative queue and reserves the right to remove content or suspend offending accounts immediately.
